# Code of Conduct

FreshClone is a community for people improving software onboarding together. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient with people of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Discuss the work and its tradeoffs; give constructive, specific feedback.
- Accept corrections and respect boundaries.
- Help keep discussions relevant and welcoming to new contributors.
- Respect others' privacy and do not publish personal information without permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual advances, personal attacks, doxxing, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, other project spaces, and public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer through a private contact method listed on [Ergis Hasani's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ergishasani). If no private method is available, open a short issue asking for a private conduct contact without including names, allegations, or sensitive details publicly.

Reports should include what happened, where it happened, and any information that helps the maintainer understand the situation. Share only information you are comfortable providing. Reports will be handled discreetly; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when action is needed to address a threat or protect participants.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban participants from project spaces. The response will take into account the severity, context, and repeated nature of the behavior. Participants are expected to comply with moderation decisions.

Maintainers are also expected to follow this policy. This document provides a community standard; it does not promise a particular response time or outcome.
